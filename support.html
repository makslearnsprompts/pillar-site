Support

Last updated: September 7, 2026

Write to glowdapp@gmail.com and we will get back to you, usually within two working days. Include your iPhone model, your iOS version, which AirPods you use and the app version from You → Version — it saves a round trip.

Pillar says my AirPods are not connected

Pillar only sees head motion while the AirPods are worn and connected to this iPhone, and only if Motion & Fitness permission is granted. Things to check, in this order:

  • Both buds in your ears — motion frames stop arriving as soon as they come out.
  • Connected to the iPhone rather than to your Mac or iPad. Play something to confirm.
  • Settings → Pillar → Motion & Fitness is on.
  • Model with motion sensors: AirPods Pro (1st, 2nd or 3rd generation), AirPods (3rd or 4th generation) or AirPods Max. Wired earbuds and non-Apple headphones cannot report head position.

I said no to Motion & Fitness and now nothing asks again

iOS only shows that dialog once. Turn it on manually in Settings → Pillar → Motion & Fitness, then start a session again.

The nudges feel wrong — too many, too few, too early

  • You → Sensitivity sets how far below your baseline counts as a slouch.
  • You → Signal & timing controls how long a slouch must last before the first nudge, the gap between nudges, and whether Pillar backs off when you ignore it.
  • If the readings feel off at their root, redo the measurement: You → Baseline. Calibration is what makes the threshold yours; a badly captured one skews everything after it.

I get no nudges when the phone is locked

The Live Activity is Pillar's main way of reaching you during a session. Check that Settings → Pillar → Live Activities is on, and that You → Background tracking is enabled. A Focus mode or Do Not Disturb can also hold cues back — and Pillar's own Quiet hours silences nudges on purpose.

Subscriptions, cancelling and refunds

All of it is in the Subscription Terms: what the free version includes, what Premium adds, the free trial, how to cancel in your Apple Account and how to ask Apple for a refund. Reinstalled the app? Use Restore purchases.

How do I delete my data?

You → Delete my data erases your history, calibration and settings and takes you back to setup. Deleting the app removes everything as well. See the Privacy Policy for what is stored where.

Feature requests and bugs

Both welcome at glowdapp@gmail.com. A description of what you expected and what happened instead is worth more than a screenshot of the crash — though the screenshot helps too.
